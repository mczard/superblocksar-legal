# Privacy Policy

**Super Blocks AR** (package `com.superblocks.ar`), a game for Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S
Effective date: 2026-11-12

## 1. Who we are
The app is published by **Dantek Solution Mateusz Czardybon** (developer: Mateusz Czardybon), referred to as "we".
Contact for privacy questions and data requests: **mateusz.czardybon@gmail.com**

This policy covers the Super Blocks AR app on Meta Quest. It is not about a website.

## 2. Summary
- We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no server of our own.
- We do not sell or share your data. We use no third-party data processors. Meta Platforms is the platform provider (see section 6).
- The app never records audio or video and never reads the headset camera images.
- Room scan, hand and head tracking data are processed on the headset only and never leave it.
- The only data that leaves the headset is your score and board name for the optional online leaderboard, sent to Meta through the Meta Platform SDK (section 4).

## 3. Data processed only on your headset
| Data | What and why | Stored? | Leaves the device? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Room scan (scene) data: tables, floor, walls, ceiling | Read only in augmented-reality modes, with your permission, to place the game board on a surface and, in the floor mode, to let falling cubes bounce off your room | Not by us. We keep only the board position you chose, as a spatial anchor held by the system on the headset | No |
| Hand tracking (optional) | Read thumb gestures to control the game when you play with hands | Not stored (memory only) | No |
| Head and controller position and input | Needed to run the game and place panels | Not stored | No |
| Settings, best scores, unlocked skins, progress, board position | Your preferences and progress | In app-private storage on the headset. Excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. Deleted when you uninstall the app | No |

The app does not access the camera images. Passthrough (seeing your room) is drawn by the Quest system, not by the app.
The app does not record audio. The release build does not request the microphone permission.

## 4. Online leaderboard (the only data that leaves the headset)
After a finished round (not in Zen mode), the app submits to the Meta Horizon leaderboard, through the Meta Platform SDK and your own Meta account:
- your score, and
- the name of the board, which is only the difficulty (`sb_spokojny`, `sb_normalny`, `sb_szybki`).

To show the Scores screen, the app reads from Meta, on demand, the ranks, scores and display names of other players and of your Meta friends. This is kept in memory only while the screen is shown. The app never saves it and never passes it on.
The app uses these Meta Platform SDK features: User ID, User Profile and Followers (friends). No room scan, camera, hand tracking or other data is sent, and we add nothing to the entry.
The Meta Platform SDK built into the app communicates with Meta's servers to submit and read leaderboard data. The app itself contacts no other server and we run no server of our own. If you are offline or Meta's service is unavailable, the score waits in a private queue on the headset and is sent later. Your local best score is always saved.
Purpose: to show rankings. Legal basis (GDPR, where it applies): performing the service you asked for (art. 6(1)(b)); you can avoid it by not finishing rounds in online modes or by playing Zen, which sends nothing.
Meta processes leaderboard data under its own terms and privacy policy; we receive no copy of it and do not store it on any server of ours.

## 5. How long data is kept
- Local data: until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- Leaderboard entries: kept by Meta on the Meta Horizon leaderboard until removed (see section 7). We hold no copy.

## 6. Sharing and processors
We do not sell your data, do not share it, and use no analytics, advertising or other processors. The only recipient is Meta Platforms (platform provider and operator of the leaderboard; the Meta Platform SDK in the app connects to Meta's servers), under Meta's own terms and privacy policy.

## 7. Your rights and how to request deletion
- **Local data** (settings, scores, skins, board position): uninstall the app, or clear its storage in the headset settings. We never receive this data, so we cannot see or delete it for you.
- **Leaderboard entries**: send an e-mail to **mateusz.czardybon@gmail.com** with the subject "Super Blocks AR data request" and say which entry to remove (board name, approximate score and date; do not send passwords). We will reply within 30 days and will remove or ask Meta to remove the entry using the tools Meta provides to developers. You can also manage your Meta account and app data through your Meta account settings.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may also have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). To use these rights, write to the e-mail above.

## 8. Children
The app is rated for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 used the leaderboard, contact us and we will have the entry removed. Use of Meta Quest by young users is governed by Meta's own age rules.

## 9. Security
We process almost nothing ourselves: data stays on your headset or goes only to Meta through the Platform SDK.

## 10. Changes to this policy
If the app's data handling changes (for example a new online feature), we will update this page and the effective date before the change ships. Material changes will also be mentioned in the store listing or release notes.
